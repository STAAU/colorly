## Now

Redesign the complete Phase 1 presentation as a premium 2026 native creative app while preserving the existing coloring engine, persistence, content models, and navigation behavior.

- Establish a warm off-white, deep-ink, lavender, coral, sky, and mint visual system with dark-mode contrast.
- Replace the standard tab presentation with an integrated floating three-destination navigation surface.
- Recompose Home editorially around one dominant artwork hero, large Continue cards, and image-led world tiles.
- Recompose Explore around featured artwork, visual category discovery, restrained search/filter controls, and large page previews.
- Present Gallery as a personal art collection with generous adaptive artwork layouts and designed empty states.
- Redesign page cards and preview surfaces to prioritize line art, minimal labels, independent favorite controls, premium spacing, and subtle press/favorite motion.
- Modernize only the chrome around the editor: clean top utilities, dominant canvas, floating tactile Fill/Brush/Eraser dock, circular palette, and refined brush-size controls.
- Keep every existing behavior intact: fill, brush, eraser, Pencil, undo/redo, reset, Photos save, zoom/pan, project autosave/resume, favorites, status, search, tabs, and navigation.
- Keep all screens responsive on iPhone and iPad, accessible, Dynamic Type-aware, Reduce Motion-aware, and legible in light/dark mode.
- Verify with a successful iOS build and focused regression inspection of engine boundaries and persistence callbacks.

## Next

- Physical-device visual QA for Apple Pencil, Reduce Transparency, and the smallest/largest Dynamic Type sizes.
- App Store screenshot art direction and final production icon refinement.

## Later

- Cloud sync, accounts, remote content, AI generation, subscriptions, and social features remain out of scope.
